About PracticalCalc

PracticalCalc is an offline-first collection of practical calculators designed for construction, DIY, workshop, mechanical, and technical tasks.

Built for tradespeople, craftspeople, and DIY enthusiasts, PracticalCalc focuses on delivering instant, reliable math right on your device. The app operates with complete privacy: all calculations and historical records remain strictly on your phone.

Core Highlights:
  • Fast Calculations: Instant results as you input your dimensions and values.
  • 100% Offline Operation: All mathematical formulas execute locally with zero network reliance.
  • Dual Measurement Systems: Full support for both Metric and Imperial units.
  • Free Utility: Accessible to everyone, supported by unobtrusive advertising.

Calculator Categories

Construction

Concrete slabs & footings, brick/block walls, roof rafters, stair stringers, and rebar estimation.

DIY & Home

Paint coverage, drywall sheet estimation, tile layout, and wallpaper requirement calculators.

Workshop & Woodworking

Board feet lumber estimation, miter & bevel angle cuts, drill pilot holes, and bolt torque references.

Mechanical & Machining

Spindle RPM & cutting speed, pulley ratios, gear speed reductions, and thermal metal expansion.

Electrical

Ohm's Law, DC/AC electrical power, wire gauge resistance, and voltage drop calculations.

Geometry & Conversions

Right triangle trigonometry, circles, cylinder volumes, slope grades, and multi-unit conversions.

Privacy & Data Transparency

We believe practical tools should respect user privacy. PracticalCalc does not maintain user accounts, does not operate a cloud database, and never transmits your calculator inputs, history, or bookmarks off-device.

Third-party advertising and consent services (Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP) operate within the app in compliance with applicable privacy regulations.

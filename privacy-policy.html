PRIVACY POLICY PracticalCalc — Construction & Workshop Calculators (com.practicalcalc.app) Effective Date: August 31, 2026 | Last Updated: August 31, 2026 1. INTRODUCTION This Privacy Policy explains how PracticalCalc — Construction & Workshop Calculators ("PracticalCalc", "the Application", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices. PracticalCalc is designed as a practical, offline-first calculation utility. The application does not require you to create a user account, does not require login credentials, and does not operate a developer-managed cloud backend to collect, synchronize, or store your calculator inputs or results. To provide free access to all calculators and tools, PracticalCalc integrates third-party advertising and consent management SDKs provided by Google. This policy distinguishes between: - Local application data processed directly by PracticalCalc on your device; and - Data processed by third-party services (Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform) for advertising, consent management, performance, and fraud prevention. 2. INFORMATION PROCESSED LOCALLY BY PRACTICALCALC PracticalCalc processes and stores calculation data locally on your physical device where applicable: - Calculator Inputs & Outputs: Numerical values, dimensions, angles, electrical inputs, machining parameters, material properties, and computed results. - Calculation History: Historical calculation records created when you execute, save, or review calculations. - Favorites & Recents: Identifiers of bookmarked calculators and recently accessed tools. - Local Preferences: Interface preferences such as measurement unit systems (Metric vs. Imperial). LOCAL STORAGE NOTICE: These records are stored locally within the application's sandboxed on-device database (Android Room) and local shared preferences. They are NEVER transmitted to developer-operated servers. The developer has no access to your individual calculation history or entered values. You can remove your local calculation history and preferences at any time by using the clear data controls in your device's Android Settings -> Apps -> PracticalCalc -> Storage -> Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application. 4. ADVERTISING — GOOGLE MOBILE ADS / ADMOB PracticalCalc uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements. When advertisements are requested, loaded, or displayed, Google may automatically process information from your device as described in Google's privacy and developer documentation. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, this information may include: - IP Address: Used to estimate approximate/coarse geographical location for regional ad serving and legal compliance. - Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), subject to operating system settings and applicable consent choices. - Device & Hardware Telemetry: Device model, operating system version, screen dimensions, network connection type, and technical characteristics. - Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Depending on the advertising configuration, Google may process ad interaction information and technical or diagnostic information for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The developer does not directly receive, maintain, or sell these device identifiers or Google's diagnostic information. Data processing by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms: - https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads - https://policies.google.com/privacy 5. CONSENT MANAGEMENT — GOOGLE UMP PracticalCalc integrates the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage applicable advertising consent choices and privacy preferences. Where required by applicable law or Google's advertising consent policies (such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland): - Users are presented with an interactive consent dialogue upon launching the application, allowing them to make choices regarding advertising-related data processing and vendor participation in accordance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). - The application respects your choices. Advertising behavior depends on the applicable consent status, device settings, regional requirements, and Google's advertising configuration. REVIEWING YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES: You can review, update, or withdraw your applicable consent choices at any time within the application by navigating to: PracticalCalc -> Settings (Gear icon) -> Privacy & Consent -> Manage Privacy Choices. 6. NETWORK CONNECTIVITY & OFFLINE FUNCTIONALITY PracticalCalc is built with an offline-first operational model: - All core calculators operate 100% offline: Mathematical algorithms, formulas, and unit conversions execute entirely on your device processor without calling external APIs. - Calculation history and saved bookmarks operate offline: Local search and record retrieval do not require an active network connection. - Network usage: An active Internet connection is used by third-party SDKs (Google UMP and Google Mobile Ads) for consent verification, ad serving, and ad-related measurement. If your device is offline or in Airplane Mode, all calculator functionality continues to operate normally. 7. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION - Local Application Data: Calculation values, history records, and favorites remain stored on your physical device until you clear application storage or uninstall PracticalCalc. - Consent Preferences: Your consent selections are stored locally in standard Android shared preferences (using IAB TCF key standards) to remember your preferences across sessions. - Advertising Data: Information processed by Google through its advertising services is retained according to Google's data retention policies and legal obligations. 8. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The application incorporates the following third-party software development kits (SDKs): - Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Provided by Google. Used for delivering in-app banner and interstitial advertisements, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. - Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Provided by Google. Used for managing user consent and privacy choices in accordance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). PracticalCalc does not integrate developer-operated analytics SDKs, third-party social media trackers, or third-party cloud synchronization tools. 9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY PracticalCalc is a general-audience utility intended for construction, workshop, and DIY calculation tasks and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. 10. USER PRIVACY CHOICES AND RIGHTS Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding information associated with your use of the application: - Control of Local Data: Because calculation records, history, and favorites are stored exclusively on your device, you have direct control over this data. You can delete locally stored information at any time via Android application settings. - Managing Advertising Consent: You can review, update, or withdraw your applicable consent choices directly inside PracticalCalc via Settings -> Privacy & Consent -> Manage Privacy Choices. - Device-Level Advertising Controls: You can manage, reset, or delete your Android Advertising ID (AAID) and configure personalized advertising settings directly in your device's operating system settings under Settings -> Google -> Ads (or Privacy -> Ads). - Google Advertising Data: For rights or inquiries concerning data collected and processed directly by Google services, please consult Google's Privacy Policy and Google account privacy controls. 11.
